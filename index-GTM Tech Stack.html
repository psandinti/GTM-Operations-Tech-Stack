How intake works

  1. SubmitAnyone in GTM files a request here. It lands on the Projects tab as New.
  2. TriageSales Ops reviews new requests weekly, confirms the priority mapping, sets effort and an owner.
  3. DecideApprove into Q4 2026 or Q1 2027, park in the backlog, or decline with a reason the requester can see.
  4. DeliverThe owner moves it through In progress to Done and updates the tech stack if a tool, cost or contact changed.

Priority score (out of 20)

Supports one of the top 3: +4. Run the business: +2. Each strategic tie: +1. Impact: 2, 4 or 6. Urgency: 1 to 3. Effort small +2, medium +1, large 0.

Scores of 13 and up are highlighted for the triage meeting.
